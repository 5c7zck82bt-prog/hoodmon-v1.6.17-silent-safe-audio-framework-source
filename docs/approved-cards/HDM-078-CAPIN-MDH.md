## Canonical printed rules text
### TRUTH AMPLIFIER — AWAKEN EFFECT
Once per turn, when one of your Research, Psychic, or Trap cards reveals an opponent’s card or reduces a Hoodmon’s ATK, gain 1 Bond.

### RECORD. DECODE. REVEAL. — ACTIVATE
You may exhaust this Tamer to search your deck for 1 Truth Network: Research or “Oracle” card, reveal it, put it into your hand, then shuffle your deck.
